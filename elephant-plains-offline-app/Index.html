GUEST INDEMNITY, ASSUMPTION OF RISK & RELEASE OF LIABILITY

I, the undersigned guest, hereby confirm and agree as follows:

Participation

I have applied to stay at, participate in, or otherwise be associated with the activities, facilities, and undertakings of Elephant Plains Game Lodge, including those conducted by its owners, management, employees, guides, contractors, or agents.

Authority (Minors)

Where applicable, I confirm that I have obtained the consent of my lawful guardian to enter into this indemnity, or that I am the lawful parent or guardian of any minor accompanying me and consent on their behalf to the terms of this indemnity.

Medical Fitness

I confirm that I am in good general health and that there is no medical, physical, or other condition which renders me unfit to participate in the activities offered by Elephant Plains Game Lodge. I accept responsibility for disclosing any relevant medical conditions.

Acknowledgement of Risk

I acknowledge that Elephant Plains Game Lodge is situated in a remote, unfenced wilderness area and that my stay, utilisation of any of the facilities available on the premises and participation in activities may involve inherent risks, including but not limited to wildlife encounters, vehicle travel, walking in natural terrain, environmental conditions, and limited access to emergency medical services. I acknowledge that certain activities and/or services may be sourced out to a third-party supplier and that Elephant Plains Game Lodge cannot be held accountable for negligence by the third-party. I accept that these risks may result in injury, illness, loss, damage, or death. As the parent and/or guardian I commit to closely supervise any minor in my care at all times, especially near water, on lodge premises in general or while participating in activities offered by Elephant Plains Game Lodge.

Water Safety:

I understand the inherent risks of making use of either of the lodge swimming pools. I understand that the swimming pools are unfenced, that there are no life guards on duty, swimming is only allowed during daylight hours and undertake, as the legal guardian and/or parent, to supervise all minors in my care.

Wildlife and General Safety

I understand that feeding and approaching wild animals which may be encountered is strictly prohibited, for the safety of myself as well as that of the animal/s. Walking between rooms and/or main lodge area is restricted to demarcated areas and may only be done on clearly marked pathways, unless advised otherwise and/or accompanied by lodge management or designated personnel.

Malaria Area

I acknowledge that Elephant Plains Game Lodge is located within an endemic malaria area and confirm that I have been advised to take appropriate preventative measures for myself and for any minor children under my care.

Compliance with Safety Instructions

I undertake to comply immediately and without question with all reasonable instructions, rules, and safety directives issued by Elephant Plains Game Lodge staff, guides, or authorised representatives, which are given for my safety and the safety of others.

Assumption of Risk and Indemnity

I voluntarily assume all risks associated with my stay at and participation in activities at Elephant Plains Game Lodge. I hereby, on behalf of myself and my heirs, executors, administrators, and assigns, release, indemnify, and hold harmless Elephant Plains Game Lodge, its owners, directors, members, employees, guides, contractors, and agents from any and all claims, demands, losses, damages, costs, or expenses of whatsoever nature, whether arising from negligence or otherwise, which may arise out of or in connection with my stay, participation, or presence on the premises.

Limitation of Liability

I acknowledge that all amenities, facilities, and activities are provided for my enjoyment and convenience. Entry onto the premises and participation in any activities occurs entirely at my own risk, and Elephant Plains Game Lodge shall not be liable for any injury, loss, damage, or death of whatsoever nature, howsoever arising.

Governing Law

This indemnity shall be governed by and interpreted in accordance with the laws of the Republic of South Africa.

Binding Effect

I confirm that I understand the contents and legal consequences of this indemnity and agree that it is binding on me. I further agree not to challenge the enforceability of this agreement.
